Each visit date contributes at most one observed initial downward crossing per threshold. “Already low” means at/below the threshold at first sight: its initial filling time is left-censored and is excluded from the observed-crossing median. “No crossing” means a date first observed above the threshold has no initial crossing in the record, and is right-censored at its last pre-entry check.
